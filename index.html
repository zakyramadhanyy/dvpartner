FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Which environments can you record in?

Laboratories, gardens, automotive workshops, manufacturing floors and homes across 9 sites in Jakarta, Bandung and Surabaya. Ask about any other setting.

What camera setup do you use?

Head-mounted cameras recording 2704 × 1520 at 30 fps with a 156° field of view.

How is quality checked?

Every recording is logged in DCOTrack with recorder, task, device and duration. Supervisors review each report, and recordings longer than a shift are flagged before delivery.

Can the data be exclusive to us?

Yes. Choose an exclusive licence, or a non-exclusive one at a lower price.

Do you provide labels or annotations?

The base delivery is raw footage with an index. Task labels and temporal segments are available as an add-on.
